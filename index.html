About the District.

The Georgia District of The Apostolic Church International, gathering believers in Austell and Gwinnett.

The Georgia District brings our assemblies in metro Atlanta together under one fellowship. We believe in the full Gospel of Jesus Christ, the baptism and gifts of the Holy Spirit, and a church that continues in the apostles’ doctrine, fellowship, breaking of bread and prayer.
